Emran is a free and open-source Islamic companion app that helps you build and sustain consistency in your daily prayers.

It is fully offline. The only network use is optional downloads you start yourself, such as extra Hadith collections and the Hifz speech model. There is no telemetry, no analytics, no advertising SDK and no account. Nothing you record can leave your device.

FEATURES

Prayer times and adhan
• Offline calculation for any coordinate or one of 164 bundled cities
• Twelve published conventions: Muslim World League, ISNA, Egyptian General Authority, Umm al-Qura, Karachi, Dubai, Qatar, Kuwait, Singapore (MUIS), Diyanet, Tehran and Ja'fari, plus a Custom mode for your own angles
• Correct across daylight saving time, because locations are stored as IANA time zones rather than fixed UTC offsets
• High-latitude rules for places where the sun never reaches the Fajr or Isha angle
• Exact adhan alarms that survive a reboot
• Per-prayer manual calibration in minutes

Prayer ledger and Qada
• Log all five daily prayers with categorised completion status
• Track and work down a backlog of missed prayers
• Consistency streaks with celebration animations

Quran
• All 114 surahs offline in Uthmani script, in a proper mushaf typeface
• Navigate by Surah, Juz or page, with search and bookmarks

Adhkar
• 55 supplications for morning, evening, after prayer, before sleep and upon waking
• Every entry includes Arabic, transliteration, translation and its source: a surah and ayah, or a collection and hadith number
• Narrated virtues where they exist, with repetition counters

Qibla compass
• Sensor fusion with geomagnetic declination correction and live calibration guidance

Dhikr counter
• Digital masbaha with custom targets, lap counting and per-count haptics

Personalisation
• 20 Material 3 themes, each with a matching launcher icon
• Light, dark or follow-system, with every theme available in both
• Home-screen widget showing the next prayer, matching your chosen theme
• Full Arabic interface with right-to-left layout and Arabic-Indic numerals

NO MONETISATION

Emran has no ads, no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. It is licensed under GPL-3.0. The author's wish is that nobody profits from it commercially, including the author.
